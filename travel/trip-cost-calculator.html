Frequently Asked Questions

Is this UK trip cost calculator free?

Yes, completely free. No sign-up, no email, no payment. Just enter your trip details and get an instant estimate.

How much does a UK trip cost per day?

A mid-range UK trip typically costs around £150-200 per person per day in London, or £100-150 per person per day in other UK cities. Budget travel can be £70-100 per day, while luxury travel can exceed £400 per day.

Is London more expensive than other UK cities?

Yes — London is significantly more expensive than other UK cities. Hotel prices in London can be 50-100% higher than equivalent accommodation in Manchester, Liverpool, Birmingham or Glasgow.

What does the estimate include?

The calculator estimates accommodation, food, local transport and attractions. It does not include international flights, long-distance trains between UK cities, or travel insurance.

How accurate is the estimate?

It is a rough guide based on typical 2026 prices. Actual costs vary widely depending on season, how far ahead you book, and your choices. Use it as a starting point for budgeting, not a precise quote.
