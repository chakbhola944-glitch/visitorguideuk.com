Frequently Asked Questions

Is this UK journey cost estimator free?

Yes, completely free. No sign-up, no email, no payment. Just pick two cities and get an instant cost comparison across train, coach, car and flight.

Are these live prices?

No. These are typical 2026 estimates based on average prices for each transport mode. Actual prices vary widely depending on how far ahead you book, the time of day, and the operator. Always check Trainline, National Express, or the operator's website for live prices.

How much does it cost to travel between UK cities?

As a rough guide: London to Edinburgh costs around £60-£120 by train (booked in advance), £25-£45 by coach, and £55-£85 in fuel by car. London to Manchester is around £40-£90 by train, £15-£30 by coach, and £30-£50 in fuel. Prices vary significantly by booking timing.

Is train or coach cheaper in the UK?

Coach is almost always cheaper than train in the UK, often by 50-70% for the same route. However, coach journeys take 1.5 to 2 times longer. For a rough comparison: London to Edinburgh coach is around £25-£45 and takes 9-10 hours, while train is £60-£120 and takes 4.5-5 hours.

Is driving cheaper than the train in the UK?

It depends on the number of passengers. For a solo traveller, train is often cheaper than driving once you factor in fuel, parking and potential tolls. For 2+ people sharing a car, driving becomes significantly cheaper than buying multiple train tickets.

Should I fly or take the train between UK cities?

For most UK city pairs, train is faster than flying once you account for airport transfers, check-in and security. Flights only make sense for very long routes (like London to Inverness) or when you're already near an airport.

How can I save money on UK train tickets?

Book Advance tickets 4-12 weeks ahead for the biggest savings. Travel off-peak (avoid weekday mornings and evenings). Use a Railcard if you qualify (16-25, 26-30, Two Together, Family & Friends, Senior). Split ticketing can also reduce costs on some routes.

What's the cheapest way to travel between UK cities?

For solo travellers, coach is almost always the cheapest option. For groups of 2 or more, driving is often the cheapest once you split the fuel cost. Train is the fastest but usually the most expensive unless you book Advance tickets far in advance.
