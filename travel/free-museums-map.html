Free UK Museums & Attractions Map

Dozens of the UK's best museums and galleries are completely free to enter, every day of the week — no ticket, no booking. Pick a city to see them on the map.

Note: Permanent collections at these museums are free — special/temporary exhibitions often carry a separate charge (typically £10-25). Some popular museums, like the British Museum, recommend booking a free timed-entry ticket online in peak season.
