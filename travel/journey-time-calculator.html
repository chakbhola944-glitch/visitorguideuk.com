Why It Matters

Planning Multi-City UK Trips

Getting a rough sense of journey time before you plan is often more useful than an exact fare or timetable — it tells you whether two cities work as a single-day trip, a comfortable overnight base, or are simply too far apart to combine without losing a day to travel.
