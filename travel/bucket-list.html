Frequently Asked Questions

Is this UK bucket list free?

Yes, completely free. No sign-up, no email, no payment. Tick off experiences and your progress is saved on your device.

How many experiences are on the UK bucket list?

30 classic UK experiences, from Changing the Guard at Buckingham Palace to walking part of the Giant's Causeway in Northern Ireland.

Is my progress saved?

Yes — your progress is saved locally on your device using your browser's storage. It will stay there when you return, but won't sync across different devices.

What if I complete the whole bucket list?

If you tick all 30 experiences, you'll see a special completion message. Very few people complete the full list — it takes years of travel to cover all four UK nations plus the smaller regional highlights.
