Frequently Asked Questions

The UK uses Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) in winter and British Summer Time (BST, GMT+1) from late March to late October. The whole of the UK — England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland — shares the same time zone.

Yes. Unlike some large countries, the UK has a single time zone nationwide, so London, Edinburgh, Cardiff and Belfast are always showing the same time.
