Practical advice for planning a UK trip with children, from choosing destinations to managing transport and pacing your days.

Overview

Planning a UK Trip with Children

The UK offers a strong range of family-friendly attractions, many of which are free, alongside compact cities that are easier to manage with children than destinations requiring extensive transport. Choosing a walkable base — such as York, Bath or Oxford — generally makes a family trip easier than trying to cover a lot of ground each day.

Balancing museum visits with outdoor time, and building in slower days between busier sightseeing days, tends to work better for families than an intensive city-hopping itinerary.

Practical

Tips for Family Travel

  • Look for family or child tickets, which are often available on both attractions and rail travel.
  • Choose accommodation within walking distance of your planned sightseeing to reduce daily transport.
  • Pack snacks and entertainment for train journeys and any queuing at popular attractions.
  • Build in downtime between busier sightseeing days, especially with younger children.
