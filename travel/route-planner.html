Frequently Asked Questions

How far is it from London to Edinburgh by road?

London to Edinburgh by road is approximately 405 miles (650 km) via the A1, taking around 7 hours 30 minutes to drive without stops.

How long does it take to drive from London to Manchester?

Driving from London to Manchester via the M1 and M6 covers about 200 miles (320 km) and takes approximately 3 hours 45 minutes without stops.

How far is Bath from London by car?

Bath is about 115 miles (185 km) from London via the M4, roughly a 2 hour 15 minute drive without stops.

How long is the drive from Manchester to Edinburgh?

Manchester to Edinburgh via the M6 and A74(M) is about 220 miles (355 km), taking roughly 4 hours 15 minutes to drive without stops.
