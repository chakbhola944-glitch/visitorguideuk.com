Overview

Planning a Trip to Bath

Bath is compact and elegant, built almost entirely from the same honey-coloured limestone that gives the city its unified Georgian look. The centre is easily walkable, with the Roman Baths, Bath Abbey and the shopping streets around Milsom Street all within a few minutes of each other.

A short uphill walk leads to the Royal Crescent and the Circus, two of the finest examples of Georgian townhouse architecture in the country, while the natural thermal springs that gave the city its name and its Roman-era importance can still be experienced today at the modern Thermae Bath Spa.

FAQ

Common Questions About Visiting Bath

How many days do you need in Bath? +

One full day covers the Roman Baths, the Royal Crescent and a walk through the Georgian streets, though staying two days allows time to relax at the modern Thermae Bath Spa as well.

Can you swim in the Roman Baths? +

No, the historic Roman Baths themselves are a museum and not open for bathing, but visitors can experience the same natural thermal waters at the nearby Thermae Bath Spa.

Is Bath easy to visit as a day trip from London? +

Yes, direct trains from London Paddington take around 90 minutes, making Bath a popular day trip, though an overnight stay allows more time to see the city without rushing.
