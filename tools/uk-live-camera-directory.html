    City facts

    About This Tool

    This page demonstrates a CCTV-style rotating camera view of UK cities. Every 8 seconds, the view changes to a different city, with the city name, region and a short description shown on screen. The timestamp updates in real time, and a weather badge gives a sense of current conditions.

    It's designed to give a sense of what a real multi-camera UK feed would feel like — useful for anyone planning a UK trip and wondering what a city looks like right now, at this time of day.

    How the rotation works

    The camera cycles through eight UK cities: London, Edinburgh, Manchester, York, Bath, Oxford, Liverpool and Glasgow. You can also click any city in the strip below the camera to jump straight to it.

    Why not real footage?

    Publicly available real-time cameras in the UK are operated by third parties, and most do not allow their streams to be embedded in another site. Rather than link you away, this tool shows a rotating camera view of each city using credited photographs.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is the UK Live City Cam real?

    No. This is a simulated CCTV-style feed designed to demonstrate how a rotating city camera view would look. The images are credited photographs of each city, shown with a camera-style overlay. The timestamps and weather are illustrative.

    Which UK cities are shown?

    The camera rotates through London, Edinburgh, Manchester, York, Bath, Oxford, Liverpool and Glasgow.

    How often does the camera change?

    Every 8 seconds, cycling through the cities in a loop. You can also pause on a city by clicking it in the list below.

    Where do the photographs come from?

    The images are sourced from Wikimedia Commons under Creative Commons licences. Credits are provided via the footer and at the source pages.
