About This Tool

This page is a CCTV-style camera wall showing simulated live feeds of UK locations. Click any camera in the list to watch it full-size. Each camera shows a different type of view — traffic on a motorway, an airport runway, a museum interior, or a city street.

The feeds are created using free stock video clips from Pexels and Pixabay, styled with camera overlays (scanlines, timestamp, REC indicator) to give the feel of a real CCTV monitor wall.

Camera types

  • Traffic — motorway and city road views with moving vehicles
  • Airport — runway and terminal views with aircraft activity
  • Museum — interior gallery and corridor views with visitors
  • City — street-level and aerial city views

Why these aren't real UK cameras

Real-time UK CCTV streams are not publicly available for embedding. This tool uses free stock footage from open-licence sources to create a realistic CCTV wall experience, with clearly labelled simulated feeds.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this real CCTV footage?

No. This is a simulated CCTV wall. The video clips are free stock footage from Pexels and Pixabay, styled with camera overlays. They are not real-time UK footage.

Where do the videos come from?

The clips are free stock videos from Pexels and Pixabay, used under their open licences. Credits are provided in the footer.

Can I click on a camera to watch it?

Yes. Click any camera in the list to load it into the main screen. The feed will play automatically.

Why do the videos loop?

Because they are short stock clips, they loop continuously to simulate a live feed.
