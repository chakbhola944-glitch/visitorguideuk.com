    City facts

    About This Tool

    This page demonstrates a rotating simulated city camera. Every 8 seconds, the view changes to a different UK city, with the city name, region and a short description shown on screen. The weather badge and the on-screen clock update live, so the page always feels active.

    It's designed to give a sense of what a real multi-city camera view would feel like — useful for anyone planning a UK trip and wondering what a city looks like right now, at this time of day.

    How the rotation works

    The camera cycles through eight UK cities: London, Edinburgh, Manchester, York, Bath, Oxford, Liverpool and Glasgow. You can also click any city in the strip below the camera to jump straight to it. Once a city is selected, its information panel updates too.

    Why not real footage?

    Publicly available real-time cameras in the UK are operated by third parties (National Highways, Transport for London, SkylineWebcams and others), and most do not allow their streams to be embedded in another site. Rather than link you away, this tool shows a simulated feed — and if you do want real cameras, our UK Live Camera Directory links to the official sources.

    What you'll see

    • A rotating city view, with a live clock, REC indicator and scanline effect for a camera feel
    • A live weather badge — simulated but realistic for the season
    • An information panel with a description and quick facts for each city
    • A progress bar showing how long until the next city

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is the UK Live City Cam real?

    No. This is a simulated live camera feed designed to demonstrate how a rotating city camera view would look. The images and weather data are illustrative. For real live cameras, see our UK Live Camera Directory, which links to official sources.

    Which UK cities are shown?

    The camera rotates through London, Edinburgh, Manchester, York, Bath, Oxford, Liverpool and Glasgow, showing a city view and information for each.

    How often does the camera change?

    The view rotates every 8 seconds, cycling through the cities in a loop. You can also pause on a city by clicking it in the list below.

    Why is the weather shown?

    The weather overlay is part of the simulated camera experience — a real city camera would often show conditions. The values are illustrative.
