Live TV & News

Watch live streams in one place — ITV News from the UK, WildEarth African safari drives, and Virtual Railfan railway cameras.

Note: These are genuine live YouTube streams by their respective broadcasters. We embed them directly so you can watch without leaving this page. If a stream is offline, please check back later.

About These Live Channels

This page brings together three live streams — one news channel and two engaging live cameras. You can watch any of them without leaving the page.

What you can watch

  • ITV News — national and regional news from the UK, live 24/7
  • WildEarth Safari — live African wildlife drives with expert guides
  • Virtual Railfan — live railway cameras showing freight and passenger trains

Why embed rather than host?

Live streaming requires significant bandwidth. By embedding YouTube's live streams, we bring you real-time content without slowing down the site — and the original broadcasters get the credit and views they deserve.

Tips for watching

  • Streams autoplay muted — tap the video to unmute
  • For the best experience, use a modern browser like Chrome, Safari or Firefox
  • On mobile, rotate your screen for a larger view
  • If a stream doesn't load, try refreshing the page

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these live streams really live?

Yes. ITV News, WildEarth Safari and Virtual Railfan all broadcast live 24/7 on YouTube. Individual streams may occasionally go offline for maintenance or switch to recorded content.

Why is a channel not loading?

Live streams can go offline or change. If a channel isn't working, try refreshing the page, or check back later.

What is WildEarth?

WildEarth broadcasts live safari drives from African game reserves, letting viewers watch wildlife in real time with expert guides.

What is Virtual Railfan?

Virtual Railfan operates live cameras at railway locations across the United States, showing freight and passenger trains passing in real time.

Who owns these streams?

Each stream is owned and operated by its broadcaster. We embed them here for convenience. Full credit is given to each channel.

Do you host the videos?

No. We do not host any video. All streams are embedded directly from YouTube. The broadcasters retain full ownership and control.
