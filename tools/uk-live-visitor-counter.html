What This Tool Shows

This page is a demonstration of a live visitor counter — the kind of widget you might see on airports, tourism boards or travel sites. It shows three things:

  • Estimated visitors in the UK right now — a live number that changes every second.
  • Arrivals and departures per minute — how many visitors are estimated to be entering and leaving the UK right now.
  • A rolling activity feed — a scrolling list of illustrative arrivals and departures from around the world.

Why isn't this real data?

There is no public, real-time feed of "everyone currently in the UK who isn't a resident." The UK does not publish this — the closest official data comes from the Office for National Statistics and the Home Office, both of which publish monthly or annual figures, not live numbers.

So this tool simulates a live counter using realistic rates, to show what such a tool could look like if the data were available.

How the simulation works

The counter starts from a base number (roughly the number of visitors the UK has on a typical day) and then applies small random changes every second — some seconds more people arrive than leave, some seconds more leave. Over a day, the total nets out to a realistic pattern.

Want the real numbers?

  • ONS — Population and migration
  • Home Office — Immigration statistics
  • VisitBritain — Tourism data

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this real live data?

No. This is an illustrative simulation. The numbers are generated in your browser and change every second to demonstrate how a live counter would work. They are not official statistics.

How many visitors are in the UK on a typical day?

According to VisitBritain, the UK receives roughly 40 million overseas visitors per year, which averages to about 110,000 per day. But at any given moment, the number actually present in the country is much higher — closer to 400,000–500,000 — because visitors stay for several days.

Why does the number go down sometimes?

Because visitors leave as well as arrive. On some seconds, more people depart than arrive, so the total drops. Over a full day, arrivals and departures roughly balance out.

Can I use this on my own website?

Yes, you can adapt this code for your own site. Just make sure you label it as illustrative or simulated, so users don't mistake it for official data.

What's the peak number of visitors in a day?

The simulation tracks a daily peak — usually in the late morning or early afternoon UK time, when arrivals from the US, Europe and Asia overlap.
