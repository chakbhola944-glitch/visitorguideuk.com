Beyond the City

Day Trips

Easy rail or car journeys that let you add castles, coastline and countryside to a city-based UK trip without changing accommodation.

Overview

Making the Most of a Day Trip

The UK's rail network makes day trips genuinely practical from most major cities — London, Manchester, Edinburgh and others all have well-connected smaller towns and attractions within an hour or two by train, without needing to pack up and move accommodation.

The best day trip for you depends on your base: London opens up Windsor, Oxford and Bath; Manchester and Liverpool are close enough to combine into a single trip; and Edinburgh gives easy access to Stirling and the edges of the Highlands.

Day Trips Across the UK

From London

Windsor Castle

From Edinburgh

A major royal castle around an hour from Edinburgh by train.

Chester

A historic walled Roman city under an hour from both cities.

From Bath

Stonehenge

A prehistoric stone circle, typically visited by car or organised tour.

From Birmingham

Stratford-upon-Avon

Shakespeare's birthplace, around 40 minutes from Birmingham by train.

From Oxford

Blenheim Palace

A grand country house and UNESCO World Heritage Site near Oxford.

More to Consider

Day Trips by City

From London

Windsor, Oxford, Bath, Cambridge and Stonehenge are all practical day trips by train or organised tour.

From Edinburgh

Stirling, Rosslyn Chapel and coastal East Lothian are all within reach for a day.

From Manchester or Liverpool

The two cities are around 45 minutes apart by train, and Chester adds a third easy stop.

From Bath

Stonehenge, Bristol and the Cotswolds are all realistic day trips from Bath.

Practical

Tips for Day Trips

  • Book train tickets in advance where possible, as prices typically rise closer to the travel date.
  • Check the last return train time before setting out, particularly for smaller towns with less frequent services.
  • Some day trips, such as Stonehenge or the Cotswolds, are easiest by organised tour if you don't have a car.
  • Pack for changeable weather, since UK day trips often involve significant time outdoors.
