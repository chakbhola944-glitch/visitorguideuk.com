Cookie Policy

Last updated: 26 August 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Visitor Guide UK ("we", "us", "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on visitorguideuk.com (the "Site"), and the choices available to you. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, to remember your preferences, and to provide information to the website's owner and, in some cases, third parties such as analytics or advertising providers.

Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the Site to function properly, such as remembering your cookie consent preferences. The Site may not work as intended without them.

Analytics Cookies

We may use analytics tools, such as Google Analytics, to understand how visitors use our Site — for example, which pages are most popular and how visitors navigate between them. This information is generally collected in aggregate and helps us improve our content.

Advertising Cookies

Our Site may display advertising through third-party networks such as Google AdSense. These networks may use cookies to serve ads that are relevant to you and to measure the effectiveness of advertising, based on your visits to this and other websites.

Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are set by third-party services that appear on our pages, such as analytics providers and advertising networks. We do not control these cookies directly, and we encourage you to review the relevant third party's own cookie and privacy policies for further detail — for example, Google's policies covering Google Analytics and Google AdSense.

Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View what cookies are stored on your device.
  • Delete some or all cookies.
  • Block cookies from being set in future.

Please note that blocking or deleting cookies may affect how parts of the Site function, and some advertising may become less relevant to you if advertising cookies are disabled.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the cookies and technologies we use. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about our use of cookies, please get in touch via our Contact page.
