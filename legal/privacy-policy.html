Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

Visitor Guide UK ("we", "us", "our") publishes travel information about the United Kingdom at visitorguideuk.com. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

We aim to collect as little personal information as possible. The information we may collect includes:

  • Newsletter sign-up: if you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect the email address you provide.
  • Contact form or email: if you contact us directly, we collect the information you choose to include in your message, such as your name and email address.
  • Automatically collected data: like most websites, our server and any analytics tools we use may automatically collect standard technical information such as your browser type, device type, approximate location, and pages visited, through cookies and similar technologies.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies to help the site function correctly, to understand how visitors use the site, and to support advertising. This can include:

  • Essential cookies required for basic site functionality.
  • Analytics cookies (such as Google Analytics) that help us understand visitor behaviour in aggregate.
  • Advertising cookies (such as those used by Google AdSense) that may be used to show relevant advertising and measure its performance.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling cookies may affect how parts of the site function.

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services to operate and improve this website, including website hosting, analytics providers, and advertising networks such as Google AdSense. These third parties may collect and process data in accordance with their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services linked from this site.

Some images on this website are retrieved from Wikipedia and the Wikimedia Foundation's public image archive, used in accordance with their applicable licensing terms.

How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain and improve the website.
  • To send newsletter updates to subscribers who have opted in, if this feature is enabled.
  • To respond to enquiries sent through our contact details.
  • To understand aggregate visitor trends and improve our content.
  • To support relevant advertising through third-party advertising networks.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Your Rights

If you are located in the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area, you have rights under UK GDPR and equivalent data protection law, including the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data, and the right to withdraw consent (for example, by unsubscribing from our newsletter at any time using the link provided in each email).

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details on our Contact page.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please get in touch via our Contact page.

Note for the site owner: this policy is a general template suitable for an independent UK travel information website using standard analytics and advertising (e.g. Google AdSense). Before publishing, please confirm it accurately reflects the specific tools, cookies, and third-party services actually used on your site, and consider having it reviewed if you plan to process significant personal data.
