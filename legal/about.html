About Visitor Guide UK

Last updated: 26 August 2026

Visitor Guide UK is an independent travel information website built to help people planning a visit to the United Kingdom find practical, useful information in one place — without wading through outdated forum posts or overly promotional tourism marketing.

What We Do

We publish destination guides, practical travel information and things-to-do recommendations covering England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. Our aim is straightforward: help visitors — whether planning their first trip to the UK or a returning traveller looking for new ideas — quickly find the information they actually need to plan a trip with confidence.

Our content covers major UK cities and destinations, historic sites and attractions, free and family-friendly activities, day trips, and practical guidance on getting around, budgeting, weather and other essentials of UK travel.

Our Approach

  • Practical over promotional. We focus on the information visitors actually need to plan a trip, not marketing language.
  • Independent. We are not affiliated with any tourism board, attraction, or travel operator. Our recommendations reflect what we believe is genuinely useful to visitors.
  • Factual. Where our guides cover history or specific facts, we aim to verify details against reliable, authoritative sources rather than repeating unverified claims.
  • Always improving. Travel information changes — new attractions open, transport options evolve, and details date quickly. We aim to review and update our content over time.

How We're Funded

Visitor Guide UK is supported through advertising, which allows us to keep our guides freely accessible to every visitor. Advertising does not influence which destinations or attractions we choose to cover.

A Note on Accuracy

While we take care to provide accurate and useful information, travel details such as opening hours, prices and transport schedules can change. We recommend confirming important details directly with the relevant attraction or service provider before you travel. See our Terms & Conditions for more on this.

Get in Touch

If you have feedback, spot something that needs updating, or simply want to say hello, we'd love to hear from you via our Contact page.
